01 / The big picture

What is media and information?

Understanding the tools and messages that connect our world.

Media and information play an important role in our everyday lives. They allow people to communicate, share knowledge, express opinions, and stay updated about current events. From newspapers to social media, different forms of media help connect people around the world.

“Media helps us understand the world—but we must also learn to understand the media.”
02 / Explore the formats

Four forms of media

Each type shares information in a different way and serves a different purpose.

📰
01 · PRINT MEDIA

Print media

Information distributed through printed materials.

Examples: Newspapers, books, magazines, and brochures.

Importance: Provides information and knowledge people can read and keep for future reference.

📺
02 · BROADCAST MEDIA

Broadcast media

Information and entertainment delivered to large audiences through electronic communication.

Examples: Television and radio.

Importance: Shares news, announcements, educational programs, and entertainment.

💻
03 · DIGITAL MEDIA

Digital media

Information created, stored, and shared using digital technology.

Examples: Websites, online articles, digital books, and learning platforms.

Importance: Makes accessing and sharing information faster and more convenient.

📱
04 · NEW MEDIA

New media

Modern forms of communication that let people interact and share content through digital platforms.

Examples: Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

Importance: Connects people, encourages creativity, and spreads information quickly.

Remember: Digital media and new media overlap, so they are not completely separate categories. Digital media is a broad term for content in digital form. New media usually refers to interactive, networked digital forms—such as social media—where people can both receive and create content.
03 / Media and society

How media affects us

Media can create opportunities, but it also comes with challenges.

✦ Positive effects

  • Provides information and education.
  • Connects people around the world.
  • Helps people communicate.
  • Spreads awareness about important issues.

! Negative effects

  • Can spread misinformation.
  • Can cause misunderstandings.
  • May distract people from responsibilities.
  • Can influence people's opinions negatively.
04 / Think before you share

Be a responsible media user

Before believing or sharing information, take time to evaluate it.

01Check who created or published the information.
02Check the date and original context.
03Look for evidence and compare with other reliable sources.
04Check whether photos, videos, or quotes are being used in context.
05If you cannot verify it, do not share it as fact.
05 / Personal insight

My reflection

What media means in my everyday life.

Media and information have become important parts of my daily life. I use them to communicate with others, learn new things, and stay updated about what is happening around me. I have also learned that information should not be accepted just because it appears online or is shared by many people. I should check the source, date, evidence, and context before believing or sharing it. For me, media is a powerful tool, and using it responsibly starts with thinking critically.
